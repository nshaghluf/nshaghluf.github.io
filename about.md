I hold a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering
from the University of Victoria, Canada.

My research focuses on intelligent decision-making
in complex and dynamic environments, particularly
multi-agent deep reinforcement learning, predictive
modeling, and autonomous systems.

My doctoral research investigated adaptive resource
allocation in wireless communication networks,
integrating machine learning, reinforcement learning,
and optimization under uncertainty and operational
constraints.

Before pursuing my doctoral research, I worked as a
Seismic Data Processing Engineer at Schlumberger,
applying computational and analytical methods to
complex geophysical problems.

My broader interests include AI generalization,
continual learning, reliable intelligent systems,
and real-world AI applications.
