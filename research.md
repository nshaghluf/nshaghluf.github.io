My research explores how intelligent agents can
learn, adapt, and make decisions under uncertainty,
limited information, and operational constraints.

## Research Interests

- Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning
- Autonomous Decision-Making
- Partial Observability
- Generalization and Transfer Learning
- Continual Reinforcement Learning
- Reliable and Trustworthy AI

## Doctoral Research

My PhD research investigated machine learning
and reinforcement learning techniques for
intelligent resource allocation in wireless
communication networks.

The work included cooperative spectrum prediction,
multi-agent reinforcement learning, and
NOMA-enabled device-to-device communication.

## Current Research Directions

I am interested in exploring generalization,
continual learning, and adaptive decision-making
in intelligent systems.
